# Brandible 20-topic blog queue

*Phase 0. Strategic queue for Brandible’s own site. Last updated: August 31, 2026.*

These topics are based on what Brandible **sells**, what the FAQs already prove buyers ask, and the problems local owners actually show up with. The four existing posts were used for voice, not as a topic list to extend.

**Mix:** 12 commercial / problem-aware · 5 educational / strategic · 3 thought leadership.

**Status:** `queued` is eligible for automation. `hold` is skipped until a human changes it. Drafted and published occupancy comes from CMS posts and open automation PRs, not from this column.

## Commercial / problem-aware (12)

### 01 — How Much Should a Small Business Website Cost?

- **Proposed title:** How Much Should a Small Business Website Cost?
- **Primary service:** Web Design & Development
- **Target reader:** Owner who knows they need a site (or a new one) and is stuck comparing $500 templates to $15k agencies
- **Search / user intent:** Commercial. “How much does a small business website cost,” “website cost 2026,” budget qualification
- **Why Brandible should write it:** Pricing is already on the FAQ ($2,000–$10,000+) but buried. A honest breakdown is high-intent content that filters tire-kickers and frames custom work vs templates. Nothing on the blog currently helps someone decide to spend.
- **Natural CTA:** Web design page + free consult for a scoped quote. Show what changes the range (pages, booking, e-commerce, copywriting).
- **Content type:** Commercial / problem-aware
- **Priority:** High

### 02 — Why Your Business Isn’t Showing Up on Google (and What to Fix First)

- **Proposed title:** Why Your Business Isn’t Showing Up on Google (and What to Fix First)
- **Primary service:** Digital Marketing (SEO / local search)
- **Target reader:** Owner who typed their service + town and didn’t see themselves
- **Search / user intent:** Problem-aware / commercial. “Why isn’t my website on Google,” “business not showing up on Google”
- **Why Brandible should write it:** The existing SEO post explains what SEO is. This one diagnoses. Map the usual local causes: weak site, no GBP, wrong NAP, no reviews, slow pages, nothing to rank. Doorstep Detailer is the proof that local visibility is buildable.
- **Natural CTA:** Digital marketing / SEO engagement, or web design if the site itself is the blocker.
- **Content type:** Commercial / problem-aware
- **Priority:** High

### 03 — Your Google Business Profile Is Doing More Work Than Your Website

- **Proposed title:** Your Google Business Profile Is Doing More Work Than Your Website

- **Content type:** Commercial / problem-aware
- **Priority:** High

### 05 — Google Ads vs SEO for Local Businesses

- **Proposed title:** Google Ads vs SEO for Local Businesses
- **Primary service:** Digital Marketing

- **Search / user intent:** Commercial comparison. “Google Ads vs SEO,” “should I run ads or do SEO”

### 11 — Is Paying for Social Media Management Worth It?

- **Proposed title:** Why Brandible Doesn’t Lock You Into a Long Contract

- **Why Brandible should write it:** “No long contracts” is on the homepage, About, services, and FAQ (month-to-month, 30 days). Almost no competitor content is this direct. It’s a conversion article for people already comparing firms.

- **Content type:** Commercial / problem-aware
